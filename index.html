How to get a helpful answer

A little context helps your Study Buddy guide you.

  1. Say your school level or the topic.
  2. Show what you have tried so far.
  3. Ask for a hint or step-by-step explanation.
  4. Try the next step yourself before asking for the answer.
Demo note: This local demo uses built-in study guidance; it is not connected to a live AI, teacher, or emergency support service. Check important work with your teacher or a trusted adult.
